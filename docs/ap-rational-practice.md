# AP rational-functions multiple-choice practice

Route: `/apps/ap-rational-practice`, listed under Functions for Precalculus.

## Scope and materials
Reviewed the packet, solutions and ca1Extra PDFs for 1.7A and 1.7B, and the packet and solutions PDFs for 1.8 and 1.9 (10 named source files total). Topics are original-domain restrictions, end behavior, horizontal asymptotes, zeros, holes, signs, vertical asymptotes and one-sided limits. Original files remain untouched and are not uploaded. Questions and exact-model SVG graphs are original; a corrected/marked source graph was not copied.

The generator contains 26 parameterized templates. Default practice has 10 no-calculator questions followed by 10 calculator-allowed questions; either part can also be selected alone. Topics can be selected. Calculator questions use numerical evaluation, tables, intersections, zeros, concentration and average rate of change; requested numerical answers round to three decimals. Students use their own calculator. These are AP-style practice items, not official College Board questions or a full exam simulation.

References consulted for calculator/no-calculator framing:
- https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/courses/ap-precalculus/exam
- https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/courses/ap-precalculus/exam/graphing-calculators

## Behavior and privacy
Each question receives one scored check: +10 correct, -5 missed. Negative net totals are intentional; no streak or score floor. Both outcomes show worked reasoning. Completed practice supports missed-question review. Local scratch paper, name/hour, Spanish with tap-English, resume codes, and shared two-reflection completion records are included. Completion includes local date/time, raw net points, maximum points, accuracy, topics and section totals. Names, answers and scores are not sent to a server or stored in browser storage.

RAT1 resume codes contain deterministic seed, settings, answer choices, initials/hour identity check and checksum. They are convenience codes, not authentication. Preserve version-1 generation order/algorithm after release or version the codec when changing it.
