# Slope Between Two Points

Route `/apps/slope-two-points`, categorized under Linear graphs and equations for Algebra 2 and Precalculus. No graph is given or requested.

Stage 1: green x-coordinates and red y-coordinates can be pointer-dragged or selected and tapped into the four slots of the slope fraction. Labels and colors reinforce y-difference over x-difference. Values retain their signs. Sources move between slots instead of duplicating. Consistent reverse subtraction is accepted; x/y mixing and inconsistent order receive targeted hints. After the setup is checked, it collapses to a green confirmed fraction. Students calculate its value themselves.

Stage 2: only two plain ordered pairs, an answer box, scratch space and keypad. No formula, coordinate labels/colors or setup boxes appear until the solution feedback. Both stages require five consecutive solved problems. Each has a fixed unscored classroom example and a post-example skill reminder.

Each problem has one retry shared by the setup and calculation checks. The first incorrect check gives a hint without changing the existing streak. A correct retry earns the same gold-star/streak credit. The second miss reveals a complete numeric difference quotient and simplified slope and resets only the current stage streak. Missing inputs trigger reminders without consuming the retry. Streak counts increment only when the slope is correct, never at setup. Completed stages remain complete.

Random points use small signed integer coordinates and rise/run magnitudes no greater than six. Include positive, negative, fractional, zero and undefined slopes; identical points are excluded. Fractions, unreduced equivalent fractions and exact decimals are accepted. Division-by-zero text is not a numeric answer; students choose Undefined for a vertical line. Keypad edits preserve the caret and selection, with minus, slash, decimal, delete and clear; Undefined is a separate button. The pen/eraser/undo/clear SVG scratchpad uses existing edge-to-edge pointer mapping and touch-action:none.

Names/hours (2,3,4,6,7,8), date and streak stay visible. No timer or student-data uploads/storage. Stars reward every solved problem. Each completed stage contributes 5 points to the mastery score; both complete earns 10/10. Reports show solved/attempted counts, best streaks, hinted retries and the local completion date/time/zone. Confidence and difficulty reflections are required before the local report image prepares automatically. Save or Share Image offers the native menu where supported, with long-press fallback for Photos.

STP resume codes use the existing local checksum/encoded-initials/hour codec with activity marker 91, stage/demo/completion flags, two stage records, retry count and completion timestamp at minute precision. They can be saved after a problem or at a stage boundary; partial work is not saved. Correct counts, streaks and completed stages restore, and the next problem starts fresh. Codes are not authentication or encryption. Full-report checkpoints preserve the original completion minute. Spanish is local, including hints, controls, reflection/report text and the undefined-slope label.
