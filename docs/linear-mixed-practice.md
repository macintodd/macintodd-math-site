# Write the Linear Equation LEVEL 5

Ten balanced, shuffled equation-writing problems. No spinner interrupts the set. After problem ten, one final number-catching game awards one extra-credit point for a match; math score remains out of ten and total may reach 11/10.

A first incorrect equation stays editable and triggers a slope-first diagnostic. If slope is correct, feedback directs the student to check their point/signs and recalculate b. One retry is allowed. Correct first attempts start at 1 point, correct retries at 0.5; each revealed hint deducts 0.2, floored at zero. Format reminders do not consume the retry. Correct equations are revealed only after a correct answer or the second incorrect mathematical answer.

Large amber learning prompts and green celebrations distinguish feedback without relying on color alone. Existing two-column scratch area, compact keypad, sticky identity, phone stacking and reduced-motion fallback remain. Final local scorecards include math score, bonus, per-problem credit and required reflections. Spanish feedback and exports are supported. No names or scores are transmitted or stored.

Validation: test-linear-mixed.mjs exercises balanced generators, first-miss retry, slope-vs-intercept guidance, hint/half-credit scoring, a single final bonus, exports and session reset; test-equation-spanish.mjs checks translated passages. Required content, type, lint and build checks also apply.
